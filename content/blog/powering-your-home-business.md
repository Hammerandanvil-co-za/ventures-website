South Africa's energy landscape is changing rapidly. With electricity costs skyrocketing and Eskom's reliability plummeting, homeowners, businesses, and industrial clients are looking for sustainable and cost-effective alternatives. Solar energy is the obvious solution, but one of the biggest hurdles is financing.

Many assume solar is too expensive, but the reality is that multiple financing options exist that make it accessible to anyone. Whether you're a homeowner looking for energy independence or a business owner trying to cut operational costs, there's a financing model that fits your needs.

Let's explore the best ways to finance your solar installation in South Africa, the pros and cons of each, and how to take the next step.

## 1. Using Your Own Savings or Funds

Best for homeowners, businesses, and industrial clients with available capital.

The simplest way to finance solar is to pay cash upfront. This means you immediately own your solar system and start enjoying the full savings without monthly repayments.

### Pros

- Immediate cost savings – No interest payments or finance fees.
- Maximises ROI – You own the system and keep 100% of the financial benefits.
- No long-term obligations – No contracts, lease agreements, or debt.

### Cons

- High upfront cost – Can be expensive, making it unrealistic for some.
- Capital allocation – Ties up funds that could be used elsewhere.

## 2. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)

Best for commercial and industrial clients who want solar with no upfront cost.

A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) allows businesses to install solar without paying for the system. Instead, you buy the energy it produces at a lower rate than Eskom.

### Pros

- No capital required – Hammer & Anvil Energy owns and maintains the system.
- Instant savings – You pay only for the electricity you use at a discounted rate.
- No maintenance costs – Hammer & Anvil Energy handles all repairs and servicing.

### Cons

- No ownership – You don't own the system unless you buy it later.
- Long-term contract – You must commit to a fixed-term agreement.
- Limited control – The system is owned by H&A for the duration of the agreement.

### Steps to Getting Solar with a PPA

1. Contact Hammer & Anvil Energy for an energy assessment and PPA proposal.
2. Sign an agreement with Hammer & Anvil Energy to lock in discounted solar energy rates.
3. Hammer & Anvil Energy installs and maintains the system on your property.
4. You pay for the electricity used at an agreed-upon rate.

Ideal for businesses & industrial operations wanting savings without upfront investment.

## 3. Solar Leasing

Best for homeowners and businesses looking for solar with low upfront costs.

A solar lease works like renting – you pay a fixed monthly fee to use a solar system instead of buying it. Unlike a PPA (where you pay for the electricity), you pay to use the system itself.

### Pros

- Low upfront cost – No need for a big capital outlay.
- Fixed payments – Predictable monthly costs.
- No maintenance worries – Hammer & Anvil Energy manages servicing and upkeep.

### Cons

- No ownership – The provider owns the system unless you buy it later.
- Long-term commitment – Contracts range from 5–20 years.
- May not cover full energy needs – Some leases limit system size.

### Steps to Getting Solar with Leasing

1. Compare Hammer & Anvil Energy's leasing options based on your energy needs.
2. Sign a leasing contract with Hammer & Anvil Energy.
3. Hammer & Anvil Energy installs the system at your home or business.
4. Pay a fixed monthly fee while enjoying solar savings.

Ideal for homeowners and SMEs who want solar benefits without a big upfront investment.

## 4. Asset Finance from Banks and Other Lenders

Best for homeowners, businesses, and industries that want to own their solar system but prefer financing.

Banks and financial institutions offer solar loans and asset finance to help clients purchase solar systems with structured repayments. Hammer & Anvil Energy works with leading financial institutions to facilitate financing solutions.

### Pros

- Immediate ownership – You own the system while paying it off.
- Access to government incentives – Claim tax rebates and incentives.
- Predictable payments – Fixed monthly repayments.

### Cons

- Interest payments – Increases total cost over time.
- Credit approval required – Not everyone qualifies.
- Loan obligations – You must repay regardless of energy savings.

### Steps to Getting Solar with Asset Finance

1. Apply for a solar loan from a bank or financing partner with help from Hammer & Anvil Energy's solar experts.
2. Receive approval and funding.
3. Hammer & Anvil Energy installs the system at your home or business.
4. Repay monthly while enjoying energy savings.

Ideal for clients who want to own their solar system without paying upfront.
