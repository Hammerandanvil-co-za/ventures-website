## What Is Cold and Hot Commissioning?

Commissioning is the process of validating that an energy plant—whether PV, BESS, or hybrid—is built to specification and operates as intended under real-world conditions. At a high level, it's divided into two critical stages:

### Cold Commissioning

Cold commissioning includes all non-energised inspections and validations. This phase verifies mechanical and electrical integrity before any equipment is powered up.

Key activities include:

- **Visual and mechanical inspections** of PV modules, cabling, and mounting structures
- **Open Circuit Voltage (Voc)** and **Short Circuit Current (Isc)** testing
- **String polarity verification** and **AC continuity checks**
- **Insulation resistance testing**
- **Labelling, fencing, and emergency systems validation**
- **BESS functionality and transformer protection relay checks**

It's all about ensuring the plant is safe, complete, and compliant—before the switch is flipped.

### Hot Commissioning

Hot commissioning begins once the system is energised. This phase validates the live operation of all components under grid-synchronised conditions.

Typical tasks include:

- **Inverter start-up and grid synchronisation**
- **Performance Ratio (PR) measurement**
- **Thermographic scanning for hotspots or failure risks**
- **SCADA signal verification** and end-to-end data flow testing
- **Reactive power and anti-islanding functionality**
- **RETEC dry runs and Eskom coordination**

In essence, hot commissioning ensures the system performs reliably, safely, and efficiently—ready for integration into South Africa's grid.

## What Is Grid Code Compliance and Why Does It Matter?

Every renewable plant in South Africa must comply with the **South African Grid Code**—a comprehensive set of technical rules set by NERSA and Eskom. Non-compliance doesn't just risk fines or delays; it can halt energy exports entirely, undermine your power purchase agreement (PPA), or prevent COD altogether.

Grid Code compliance requires:

- Accurate system modelling (e.g., via DIgSILENT PowerFactory)
- Reactive power capability assessments
- Load flow and rapid voltage change studies
- Full validation of SCADA/RTU/PPC functionality
- Compliance matrix documentation for RETEC and Eskom

Grid Code compliance isn't just a box-ticking exercise. It's a strategic imperative for operational readiness, long-term performance, and regulatory legitimacy.

## Why Commissioning and Grid Code Compliance Must Be Done Right

If commissioning is rushed or poorly executed, the consequences are severe:

- **Eskom rejections and failed RETEC submissions**
- **Unplanned rework and costly delays**
- **Inverter or BESS malfunctions under load**
- **Risk to on-site personnel due to overlooked safety issues**
- **Delayed revenue recognition from the offtaker**
- **Increased long-term O&M costs due to poor initial validation**

Think of commissioning as your project's final exam. Fail it, and everything else stalls.
