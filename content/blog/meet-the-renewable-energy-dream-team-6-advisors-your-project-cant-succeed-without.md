In a renewable energy project, the quality of your technology matters—but the quality of your team defines the outcome.

From utility-scale solar farms to commercial rooftop systems, every project is a multifaceted operation requiring input from technical, legal, financial, and environmental specialists.

Here are the 6 essential advisory roles every RE project needs to succeed:

## 1. EPC/M (Engineering, Procurement & Construction Management)

Manages project design, procurement, and site execution—ensuring quality, compliance, and efficiency.

***Why it matters:*** They reduce construction risks, optimise timelines, and deliver on budget.

## 2. O&M Advisors (Operations & Maintenance)

They develop maintenance protocols, manage warranties, and track performance KPIs.

***Why it matters:*** A well-maintained project is a profitable one. Uptime = returns.

## 3. Financial & Tax Advisors

They craft the capital stack, tap into incentives, model cash flows, and structure bankable deals.

***Why it matters:*** Investors and lenders want clarity. Good financial design secures funding.

## 4. Legal Advisors

They handle everything from permits to PPA negotiations and contract reviews.

***Why it matters:*** Projects live and die by the strength of their contracts and compliance.

## 5. Environmental & Permitting Consultants

They prepare EIAs, conduct stakeholder engagement, manage licensing (WULA, SPLUMA, etc.), and ensure regulatory alignment.

***Why it matters:*** Delays from non-compliance are expensive—and avoidable.

## 6. Technical Advisors / Owner's Engineer (OE)

Acting on behalf of the owner, the OE verifies that technical specs are met and that timelines are adhered to.

***Why it matters:*** Independent quality control protects your investment and your reputation.
